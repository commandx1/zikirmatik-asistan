```text
Zikirmatik Asistan is a dhikr app designed to help you keep a regular dhikr and dua practice. It counts your dhikr, sets up your daily wird (your dhikr routine), lets you reach a shared goal with your loved ones, and offers sourced suggestions when you decide what to recite.

DHIKR COUNTER
• Count with a single tap; use goal mode or free mode
• Pick up where you left off and see your rounds and progress
• The Arabic text, transliteration and meaning of each dhikr on the same screen
• Add your own dhikr, edit them, and mark favorites

DAILY WIRD
• Start with ready-made wird programs for morning, evening and after prayer, or build your own program
• Guided session: you move to the next dhikr, the app keeps the count
• Build a wird with AI: write your intention and get a program suggestion split across days
• Keep your routine with reminders

DHIKR CIRCLE
• Create a free circle with your family or friends and let them join with an invite code
• Everyone's dhikr is added into one shared total; you reach the goal together
• See the circle's progress and members on a single screen

AI GUIDE
• Write your intention or your mood; get dhikr and dua suggestions together with the reasons for them
• Assistant Chat: answers to your questions with book and page references
• Suggestions are based on the works in the app's source library
• The assistant does not issue religious rulings (fatwa); for matters that require a ruling, it suggests consulting a qualified scholar

SPECIAL DAYS
• Countdowns for the blessed nights (Kandil), the three holy months and important days of the Hijri calendar
• Quick access to details of the upcoming day and to content suited to that day

DHIKR LIBRARY
• Collections organized by topic: daily dhikr, prayer, dua, protection and more
• The library is free

STATISTICS AND STREAK
• Daily, weekly and monthly totals; daily average and completion rate
• Track your daily streak and earn badges

HOME SCREEN WIDGET
• See today's dhikr total and your daily streak on your home screen
• With the Wird widget, follow today's progress and your next dhikr, and return to your session with one tap

PERSONALIZATION
• More than 20 light and dark themes, different fonts
• Turkish and English language support

MEMBERSHIP AND PURCHASES
The counter and the dhikr library are free. You can create your first dhikr circle for free (up to 5 people); advanced features such as multiple circles at the same time, larger circles and the Wird widget require a Premium membership; AI features work with credits, and extra credits can be purchased in the app.

Bring your dhikr practice into a more regular and sustainable routine with Zikirmatik Asistan.
```
